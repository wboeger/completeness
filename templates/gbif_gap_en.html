The analysis queries the GBIF API in real time. For taxa with many species (e.g. Coleoptera), it may take several minutes. The page updates automatically until the result is ready. Comparison is done by canonical binomial in lowercase; nomenclatural discrepancies between GBIF and CTFB may generate false positives in the absent list.
